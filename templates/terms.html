Terms & Concepts

Plain-English reference for the money management, risk rules, and indicators used by the system.

1. Money & Value
Portfolio Value (Net Worth) Balance
The full current market value of your account: your available cash plus the live value of any open crypto positions.
Example: $9,000 cash in wallet + a coin currently worth $1,050 = $10,050 total portfolio value.
Unrealized vs. Realized P&L Performance
Unrealized P&L is the paper gain or loss while a trade remains open. Realized P&L is the permanent, banked cash profit or loss after the trade has closed.
Example: A property that gains value on paper vs. the cash in your bank after you complete the sale.
Accuracy Rate (Win Rate) Ratio
The percentage of completed trades that ended with positive profit: (Winning Trades / Total Closed Trades) × 100.
Example: 7 profitable trades out of 10 completed trades equals a 70.0% accuracy rate.
2. Capital Protection
Stop-Loss (SL) Safety: 2.0%
An automated market sell order executed if a coin drops 2% from entry, preventing small pullbacks from turning into large drawdowns.
Example: An emergency brake stopping a vehicle automatically if the path ahead collapses.
Take-Profit (TP) Target: 3.0%
A preset exit price (+3%) where the bot automatically closes the position to lock in cash profits.
Example: Selling concert tickets the instant they reach your desired price, avoiding a last-minute drop.
Break-Even Protection Trigger: +1.5%
When an open position reaches +1.5% profit, the stop-loss price moves to your purchase price. Once triggered, the trade cannot lose capital.
Example: Removing your initial bet from the table once ahead, playing strictly with accumulated profit.
1% Risk Rule Sizing
Position sizing is calibrated so that if a stop-loss is hit, the maximum loss is capped at 1% of the portfolio ($100 on a $10,000 account).
Example: Never risking all your eggs in one basket; if one cracks, 99 remain intact.
Daily Circuit Breaker Emergency: 3.0%
If total daily losses reach 3%, all automated trading halts until the following day to prevent emotional over-trading during flash-crashes.
Example: A residential circuit breaker tripping to stop an electrical overload.
3. Strategy & Signals
Timeframe (1-Hour) Resolution
Each candlestick chart interval spans 1 hour. This timeframe filters out short-term 5-minute market noise and reduces false breakout entries.
Example: Evaluating seasonal climate trends rather than minute-by-minute wind changes.
Moving Averages (EMA 50 / 200) Trend Filter
Smooth lines tracking average price over 50 and 200 hours. The bot automatically vetoes any buy orders if the market is trading below the 200 EMA.
Example: Only sailing with the tide, never steering into an opposing current.
RSI & MACD Momentum
Mathematical indicators that gauge whether an asset was oversold (overshot to the downside) and whether momentum has turned bullish.
Example: A rubber band stretched downward snapping back toward equilibrium.
4. Environment
Paper Trading (Testnet) Simulation
Executing simulated trades against real-time exchange data using test currency, validating execution without risking real funds.
Example: A certified flight simulator used by commercial pilots before live flight.
