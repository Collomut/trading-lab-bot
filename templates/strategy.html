Strategy & risk

A readable explanation of the current configuration and what its controls do. Indicators are rules, not proof of a profitable edge.

Learning and simulation onlyThe current strategy has not been proven on unseen data. Backtests in this project omit important execution costs and should not be used to decide to trade real money.

Signal rules

  • EMA compares a short and long price average for a possible trend change.
  • RSI estimates recent upward versus downward price movement.
  • MACD compares price averages to estimate momentum changes.
  • The combined strategy requires agreement from at least two of these indicators and may filter buys using a trend and volume check.

How to read the process

  1. The app downloads recent price candles for each configured pair.
  2. It calculates indicators and creates a buy, sell, or hold signal.
  3. Risk checks may block a new position. An accepted signal submits a market order to the configured exchange environment.
  4. Open exit levels are checked by the running app. They are not placed at the exchange.
  5. Results are written to local files, so a persistent deployment volume is needed to retain them across redeploys.

What has not been demonstrated

  • That the strategy earns money after fees and realistic execution costs.
  • That performance generalizes to unseen periods and different market conditions.
  • That a tracked position always matches the exchange account after a restart or interrupted order.
  • That app-monitored stops execute during service or network outages.
