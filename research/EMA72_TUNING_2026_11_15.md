**No tweak makes the strategy robustly profitable.** The only defensible change is
v4 (skip entries when ATR14 > 2.5% of price), which trims drawdown slightly at
equal profit factor. Train drawdown remains ~6%, above the project's 5% screen.

The 3-loss halt is the dominant performance killer (as previously audited): at
cap 4-5 the strategy is clearly positive in 2021-2025, **but the 2026 review
slice is negative under every cap**. That is the honest, uncomfortable result:
the edge did not persist into the most recent period.

## Conclusion

- Do **not** treat any of these tweaks as a validated profitability improvement.
- The ATR-band filter is a marginal robustness option, not a fix.
- The correct next step is forward measurement via the halt-free shadow ledger,
  not further in-sample tuning on reused history.
